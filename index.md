1. Introduction

SherwoodTales (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our application or services that interact with Pinterest through the Pinterest API.

Our primary purpose is to help manage Pinterest content, including the bulk editing and updating of Pin descriptions, in connection with the SherwoodTales Pinterest business account.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, our application may access or process the following information through the Pinterest API:

Pinterest account information: Basic account information made available through the permissions you grant.

Pin information: Pin IDs, titles, descriptions, and other content necessary to identify and update Pins.

Authorization data: Access tokens or other authentication information required to securely connect to Pinterest.

Technical information: Limited technical data necessary to operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the application.

We only access information necessary to provide the intended functionality of the application and within the scope of the permissions granted by the user.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information accessed through the Pinterest API solely for legitimate application purposes, including:

Identifying Pins selected for editing.

Updating Pin descriptions in bulk at the user's request.

Processing and applying description changes submitted by the user.

Maintaining the functionality, security, and reliability of the application.

Troubleshooting technical issues and preventing unauthorized access.

We do not use Pinterest data for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Authentication credentials and access tokens, if stored, are protected using appropriate security measures and are accessible only when necessary for the application's operation.

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain security, or meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not retain Pinterest content longer than necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information or Pinterest user data.
